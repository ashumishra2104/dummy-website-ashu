The Journey

A Story of Building at Scale

Every great product thinker has an origin story. Ashu's begins not in a product team, but on a factory floor — and that foundation changed everything.

2015 – 2017

The Foundation

Procurement Engineer · Eon Electric Limited, Delhi

Before product, there was process. As a Mechatronics engineer, Ashu learned the unglamorous but essential craft of making supply chains work — coordinating between Sales, R&D, QA, and Finance to source components that keep the lights on. Literally.

This wasn't just a job. It was a masterclass in understanding how organisations actually function from the inside — the kind of ground-level empathy that most PMs never develop.

2018 – 2020

The Pivot

PGDM (Marketing) · IMT Ghaziabad

A deliberate choice: combine technical depth with business strategy. At IMT Ghaziabad, Ashu connected the dots between engineering precision and market thinking. The question he kept asking: "What if we could build systems that solve problems at scale?"

The answer led him to product management.

Mar 2020 – Apr 2022

The First Build

Product Manager · ElasticRun, Pune

First PM role. Immediately thrown into the deep end of B2B logistics. Ashu didn't just learn the craft — he applied it to problems that mattered: managing ₹200Cr/month in revenue, automating the sales app to cut manpower needs by 90%, and building data dashboards that gave leadership real-time operational clarity.

₹200Cr/month revenue managed 90% manpower reduction 5% monthly EBITDA growth
Apr 2022 – Dec 2023

The Scale-Up

Senior Product Manager · ElasticRun, Pune

Promoted. Now leading a team. Ashu built the P&L Ecosystem from scratch — establishing KPIs across 20+ warehouses and driving profitability metrics that earned his team the company's "Team of the Year" award.

He also shipped products that changed user behaviour: SMART Recommender, SMART Basket, and a Medal Framework for sales gamification — lifting margins from 4.5% to 7.5% and growing items per invoice from 7 to 12.

🏆 Team of the Year Margin: 4.5% → 7.5% ₹40Cr working capital unlocked ₹2Cr/month logistics savings
Jan 2024 – Present ● Live

The Builder

Senior Product Manager · Medikabazaar, Gurgaon

Ashu didn't join to maintain — he joined to build. From scratch, he established Medikabazaar's entire fintech vertical: integrating 100+ banks, 5 payment gateways, and an open-router orchestration system that now handles ₹300–400Cr in invoices monthly at a 99.5% payment success rate.

But the headline achievement? An end-to-end procurement platform that unlocked USD 130M of working capital through intelligent inventory orchestration. Not a tweak. Not an iteration. A platform built from zero.

He also shipped a React Native field sales app (₹200Cr/month in sales), a 1.1M SKU catalog platform with semantic search, and a machine-learning sales enrollment system managing ₹350Cr of monthly orders.

$130M Working Capital 99.5% Payment Success 1.1M SKU Platform 100+ Bank Integrations
